FAQ

How do I reset my password?

On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password" and follow the link sent to your email.

Is my data synced between devices?

Yes. Sign in with the same account on your iPhone and iPad and your data stays in sync.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings in the app and tap "Delete Account". This permanently removes your account and data.

Something isn't working. What should I include in my email?

Your device model, iOS version, and a short description or screenshot of the problem.
