Freight Manager

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

In short

  • We only collect what the app needs to work: your account, your organisation's details and the freight data you and your team enter.
  • We do not sell your data, show adverts, use analytics or tracking, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Your data is stored securely with Google Firebase in the European Union and is only visible to members of your organisation.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time from inside the app.

Who we are

Freight Manager (“the app”) is provided by Ozan Bakis (“we”, “us”). We are responsible for the personal data processed through the app.

Contact: ozanbakis@gmail.com

Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account we collect your name, email address and a password. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us. Each account has a user ID that links it to your organisation.

Organisation information

Details your organisation adds, such as its name, logo, address, phone number, email, website, VAT and company numbers, bank details (for example IBAN and SWIFT/BIC) used on invoices, and the names and email addresses of team members and people who have been invited.

Business data you enter

Information you and your team enter or import to run your freight business, which may include personal data about other people:

  • Loads and haulage jobs: customer, shipper and consignee names, contact people, phone numbers, email addresses and addresses; cargo details; vehicle, trailer, container and air waybill numbers; driver names and phone numbers; and status history.
  • Saved customers and their contact details.
  • Customer invoices and supplier invoices, including amounts and payment status.
  • Delivery notes, including the name of the person receiving the goods and their handwritten signature.
  • Files you add, such as photos, scans and PDFs of invoices or transport documents.

Customer tracking codes

Customers can view their loads with a 14-digit code without creating an account. The code is saved on their device so they don't need to enter it again. If they sign a delivery note, we store the name they enter and their signature.

Camera and photos

The app only uses your camera or photo library when you choose to scan or select a document. Text in invoices is read on your device using Apple's built-in text recognition. Images are only uploaded if you save them to a supplier invoice or a load.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, browsing history, advertising identifiers or health data, and we do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

How we use information

We use the information only to provide the app's features:

  • creating and securing your account, and keeping your organisation's data in sync across your team's devices;
  • managing loads, haulage, customers, invoices, delivery notes and documents;
  • letting your customers follow the loads you choose to share with them;
  • creating PDF and Excel files when you export or share them;
  • keeping an activity log for your organisation's owner.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or automated decision-making, and we do not sell it.

Legal basis (UK and EU users)

We process personal data because it is necessary to provide the service you have asked for (performance of a contract), and for our legitimate interest in keeping the app secure and working. For business data your organisation enters about other people, such as customers, drivers or receivers, your organisation decides what to enter and is responsible for having a lawful basis to do so. We process that data on your organisation's behalf.

Who can see your data

  • Your organisation: members of your organisation can see its loads, customers, invoices and files. Only the owner can see the activity log.
  • Your customers: a customer with a valid tracking code can see only the loads linked to that code, and only until the code expires. Contact details, goods values, internal notes and driver phone numbers are never shown to customers.
  • Service providers: we use Google Firebase (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC) for sign-in and data storage. Google processes the data on our behalf under its data processing terms. When you view a route map, city names are sent to Apple Maps to find their location.
  • When you choose to share: if you share a PDF, Excel file or tracking code, or open a route in Apple Maps or Google Maps, the information goes to the app or person you choose.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if the law requires it.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore in the European multi-region (eur3), with copies kept on your devices so the app works offline. Google may process some data outside the UK or EU, for example for sign-in. Where it does, it relies on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep data

We keep your data for as long as your account or organisation exists. Customer tracking codes stop working when they expire or are revoked.

WhenWhat happens
You delete your accountYour account is deleted. If you are an organisation's owner, the organisation and all its loads, customers, invoices, files, codes and logs are deleted too.
You leave or are removed from an organisationYou lose access to its data. Your name may remain on records you created, such as “Created by” and the activity log.
An item is deletedIt is removed from our database straight away and from devices the next time they sync.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by Google. Access is restricted by security rules so that only members of your organisation can read its data, and customers can only see the loads their code covers. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to a data protection authority. In the UK this is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

  • You can edit your details and delete your account in the app, under Organization → Account → Delete Account.
  • Your organisation can export its data to Excel from Statistics & Exports.
  • If your details were entered by a company using Freight Manager, for example as their customer or driver, please contact that company first. You can also contact us and we will help.

Children

Freight Manager is a business app and is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, we will also let you know in the app.
